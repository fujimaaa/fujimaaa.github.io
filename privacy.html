aitool Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

aitool is a personal tool used by one person, its developer, to publish their own artwork and videos to their own social media accounts. It is not currently offered to the public.

What the app receives

When the owner signs in with TikTok, Instagram or YouTube, the app receives a login token and basic profile information (such as the account display name). This is stored only on the owner's own computer.

How it is used

Tokens and profile information are used only to publish content the owner has chosen to post. Videos and photos are sent to the platform only when the owner approves a post.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app collects no data about other people.

Your control

Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's app-permission settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security & permissions → Apps and services).
